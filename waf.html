The Game

Gameplay Overview

Core Loop

Hit a mole and look for the next!

Win Condition

Reach the highest score!

Key Mechanic

There are two types of moles, the mole with a pie and X will take away from your score & the normal mole will increase your score count.

Inspiration

When brainstorming a simple yet fun game everyone knows that is easy to port to VR we quickly came up with Whack-A-Mole. It is something the majority of people has played once at an arcade and can be easily picked up.

Under the Hood

Programming & Systems

Unity Blender Adobe Photoshop Plastic SCM

Lead Programmer & Designer

We wanted to replicate the feeling of arcade style Whack-a-Mole while using the advantages VR has to offer. To spin off from the real life we decided that the moles would instantiate all around the player alerting them where they are. Once the player turns around they must aim the VR hand towards the mole!
